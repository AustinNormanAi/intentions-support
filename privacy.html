Privacy Policy

Intentions: Daily Micro Habits, for iPhone. Last updated 17 November 2026.

The short version. There's no account to make. Your goals, plans and progress stay on your phone. The AI is off until you say yes. If you turn it on, we send Anthropic's Claude your goal, your answers, today's step and how your week went, through OpenRouter, so it can draft your plan and the examples in your lessons. Our server passes your words along and doesn't keep them. It keeps a random ID for your phone and a count of how many AI requests it made each day, nothing else. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Everything you put into the app is stored on your iPhone, in the app's own storage:

  • the life areas you pick, your goal sentences and the "why" behind them
  • your plans, daily steps, habits and the routine you tie them to
  • completed steps, streaks, XP, levels and your tree
  • lesson progress and your answers to lesson questions
  • reminder times and settings
  • the name you type in Me, if you give one

None of this is sent to us or anyone else, except the parts listed in the next section when the AI is on. It is not synced to iCloud. We can't see it.

When the AI is on

The AI is optional. Before your first plan is drafted, the app tells you what it sends and to whom: tap Draft my plan to use it, or I’ll write it myself to skip it. You can turn it off or on any time in Me › Let AI draft my plans and lesson examples. With it off, you write your plan yourself, the app uses its own built-in lines where it needs them (such as next week's focus and the lesson examples), and nothing is sent.

With it on, the app sends this text, over an encrypted connection:

WhenWhat is sent
You ask the AI to draft or redraft a planthe life area, how many years out, your goal, why it matters, where you are now, and how many minutes a day
A new week starts on your planyour goal, why it matters, last week's title, your habit's name, how many days you did it, and the minutes a day
Now and then when you open the app, when your next lessons don't have examples yetto write the examples in your next few lessons: the lesson's own text, plus your life area, your goal, today's step (its name and minutes) and the routine you tied it to

Nothing else is sent. The name you may type in Me stays on your phone. The app never has your email, contacts, location or device identifiers, so it can't send them.

Who handles it

  • Our server, which runs on Supabase (a hosting service). It checks the request, counts it toward a daily limit, passes the text to the AI, and sends the answer back to your phone. It does not save the text you wrote and does not write it to its logs.
  • OpenRouter, which every request goes through on its way to the AI.
  • Anthropic's Claude, the AI that writes your plan and the examples in your lessons.

What reaches OpenRouter and Claude is the text listed above and nothing that identifies you: no ID, and not your phone's internet address (the request comes from our server). We ask OpenRouter to use only providers that don't train models on your words. OpenRouter and those providers may still keep a request for a limited time under their own policies, for example to watch for abuse. We share data only with these services, only to provide the AI feature, and we expect them to protect it at least as well as this policy does.

What our server keeps

  • A random ID for your install. The first time the AI is used, the app quietly creates an anonymous user on our server, with no name, email or password. Its random ID lets the server tell one phone's requests from another's. The app keeps the matching sign-in token in your phone's keychain.
  • A daily count of AI requests for that ID (the date and a number), so one phone can't run up unlimited AI use. Each install can make 20 requests a day.
  • Security and server logs kept by Supabase, which can include the internet address and device type a request came from. We use these only to keep the service running and secure.

The random ID and daily counts contain none of the text you wrote. Nothing deletes them on a schedule yet, and the app has no button to delete them.

No ads, no tracking

Intentions has no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting services and no third-party code that collects data. We don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell or share your data for advertising.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone by iOS. They don't go through our server, and the app asks your permission before sending any.

Your choices, and deleting your data

  • Turn the AI off in Me. Nothing more is sent from then on, and lessons go back to their standard examples.
  • Put a goal away by archiving it in Plan (the … menu). It leaves your plan but stays on your phone until you delete the app.
  • Delete everything on your phone by deleting the app. That removes your goals, plans and progress for good; we have no copy to restore them from.
  • Ask about what our server keeps by emailing us at the address below. The app doesn't show you its random ID and the ID holds nothing about you, so we usually can't tell which one is yours from an email. We'll reply and tell you what we can do.

Age

Intentions is meant for people aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the AI, turn it off in Me and email us; we'll delete anything we can match to them.

Changes

If what the app does with data changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the App Store.
