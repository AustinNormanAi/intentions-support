Support

Intentions turns a goal you care about into one small step a day. You write what you want in 1, 5 or 10 years and why, get a plan that runs down to this week and today, and do a 5- or 10-minute step tied to something you already do.

Need a hand? Email austinnormanads@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and the app version (it's at the bottom of the Me tab).

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for. Your goals and progress are kept on your phone.

How do I mark a step as done?

On Today, press and hold the step for about a second until it fills, then let go. There's no timer: do the step whenever it fits, then hold to mark it.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing breaks. The rule is "never miss twice": one missed day pauses your streak, and doing your step the next day carries it on. Two missed days in a row start the streak over. Your tree keeps every day you've done either way.

Do I have to use the AI?

No. When your plan is ready to draft, tap I'll write it myself and write each line. Even with the AI, you can change any line it wrote. If you said yes earlier, turn it off in Me › Let AI draft my plans and lesson examples. With it off, nothing you write is sent anywhere.

What does the AI see?

Only what it needs. For your plan: the life area, your goal, why it matters, where you're starting from and your minutes a day. For each new week: your goal, why it matters, last week's title, today's step and how many days you did it. For lesson examples: your goal, today's step and when you do it. It goes from our server to Anthropic's Claude, through OpenRouter, and our server doesn't keep it. The privacy policy has the details.

How do reminders work?

You get a reminder at the time you picked for your step, and an evening reminder only if a step is still open that day. Turn either one off in Me › Reminders. If you see no reminders, check that notifications are allowed for Intentions in the iPhone's Settings app.

What is habit stacking?

Once a habit has held for 14 days in a row, you can claim a reward that lets you add a second small habit right after the first one.

Can I skip today's lesson?

Yes. The one-minute lesson comes after your steps, and skipping it is one tap. Your streak is safe.

How do I put a goal away?

Open the goal in Plan, tap the … button, and choose Archive. If you reached it, choose Mark as done instead.

How do I delete my data?

Everything you've entered lives on your phone, so deleting the app deletes it for good; we can't restore it. If you used the AI, our server keeps only a random ID and a daily count of AI requests, with none of your words. The app has no button to delete those yet; email us and we'll tell you what we can do.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone only.
